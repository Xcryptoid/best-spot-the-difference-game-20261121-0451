Spot the Difference — Five Worlds: five original hand-drawn scenes (a moonlit observatory, a desert oasis at dusk, a snowy mountain lodge, an underwater coral reef, and a clockwork toy workshop), sixty differences to hunt — twelve per scene, fair but hidden by design. Two pictures hang side by side; every find earns a gold ring and a check, a miss costs 15 points and 3 seconds, and a hint (H) costs 25 points on a 15-second cooldown. A countdown, a live counter of differences left, a time bonus on every cleared round, and synthesized WebAudio sounds with a music loop. No libraries, no network, no build step — plain canvas and vanilla JavaScript.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Click a difference on either picture, press H for a hint, R to restart, M to mute. Staged frames for the screenshots live behind `?shot=start|play|near|win`.
